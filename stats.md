# **Mahabharata Trump Card Game \- 40 Character Stat Database**

This reference guide contains balanced 1–100 stat ratings for all 40 characters in the game roster.

## **📊 The 4 Core Stats**

1. ⚔️ **Combat Skill** — Overall weapon mastery, martial arts, and battle fighting technique.  
2. 💪 **Strength** — Raw physical power, muscle, durability, and weapon weight capability.  
3. 🧠 **Strategy** — Wisdom, intelligence, tactics, political cunning, and mind games.  
4. 🦁 **Courage & Spirit** — Heroism, inner fire, resilience, moral strength, and defiance against impossible odds.

## **🎮 Gameplay Balance Highlights**

* **Kids can easily pick winner stats**: If an 8-year-old calls **"Strength"** with *Bhima (100)* or *Balarama (99)*, they feel instant satisfaction.  
* **Underdogs can win**: If someone holds *Draupadi* or *Abhimanyu*, calling **"Courage & Spirit"** (100) beats almost anyone in the deck\!  
* **Brain over Brawn**: *Shakuni* and *Vidura* have lower physical scores, but calling **"Strategy"** (100 and 98\) lets them outsmart supreme warriors.
